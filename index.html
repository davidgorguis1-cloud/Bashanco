How can we help?

Support for the Basha & Co dine-in ordering app at Craigieburn Central, Victoria.

Choose your table

Open the app, select the table number displayed at your table, then tap Order & Pay. Check your table number before submitting an order.

Menu and payments

The app takes you to our existing online ordering service to browse the live menu and complete checkout. Please ask our staff if anything does not load correctly.

Kitchen orders

If you have paid but are unsure whether your order arrived, please speak to our restaurant team and show your order confirmation.

Contact and support

Speak with our team

For help with your table, order, payment, accessibility, or app feedback, speak with our restaurant team at Basha & Co. If you need urgent help while dining, please ask a staff member before placing another order.

About the app

Simple dine-in ordering
