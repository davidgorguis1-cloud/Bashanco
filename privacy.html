Privacy Policy

Basha & Co dine-in ordering app · Updated 31 October 2026

This policy explains how the Basha & Co iPhone app works and how personal information may be handled when you use the connected online ordering service. If you have privacy questions, contact us at Davidgorguis1@gmail.com.

1. What the app does

The Basha & Co app allows customers dining at our restaurant to choose a table and open our online menu and checkout. The app sends users to the external MiPOS/Menu247 ordering website at bashaco.menu247.au to place orders and make payments.

2. Information handled in the iPhone app

The native app does not require you to create an account or enter your name, email address, phone number or payment-card details. Your selected table number is held in the app's active session so it can open the corresponding online ordering page. The native app itself does not process card payments.

3. Information handled by the ordering and payment services

When you open the linked ordering website, the service and its payment providers may process information needed for browsing, checkout and order fulfilment. Depending on the features you use, this could include your table number, order contents, contact details you choose to provide, payment-related information, transaction details, and technical information such as IP address or cookies. Payments take place through the connected checkout rather than within the native app.

Those providers may use information to process your order, facilitate payment, help prevent fraud, operate their services and comply with legal obligations. Their own privacy policies and checkout notices should be consulted for details about their collection, use, retention and disclosures.

4. How we use information

Information available to our restaurant through the ordering service may be used to prepare and fulfil your order, provide customer support, resolve transaction problems, and meet applicable business and legal requirements. We do not claim that information handled by an external service is stored only in the native app.

5. Information sharing

Order and payment processing involves our connected ordering service and its payment partners. We do not sell personal information through the native app. Information may be handled by service providers involved in delivering the ordering and payment functionality.

6. Security and retention

Checkout and card processing are handled by the connected ordering and payment services. The native app does not ask you to type card details into it. Data held by external services is subject to their security measures and retention practices. We keep restaurant records for operational and legal purposes where applicable.

7. Your choices and questions

You can browse the app without creating an in-app account. If you have questions about your information or wish to make a privacy-related request, email Davidgorguis1@gmail.com. We will address requests relating to information we control and, when appropriate, direct you to the ordering or payment provider for information they control.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if our app or services change. The latest version will be displayed on this page with an updated date.
